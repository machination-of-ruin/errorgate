### Done, in order

1. **Organic shapes** (`4f3701429d`): the playable ground is a blob with the same area as the nominal size (`shape: organic`, `shape: rect` for the old square), a box 1.5 times bigger holds it; shore, streets, lots, trees and the border all follow the edge of the shape. Back lanes in the village (loops, not only dead ends), trees along the village roads, street lights (`LightPostSmall`, already existing) along the city sidewalks.
2. **The web works** (this commit): `worldGraph` prototype (`Default`: Village - tunnel - Snow City), `WorldGate` entities (look like the bunker hatch, named *hatch* as the bunker's), gates placed at the ends of the main roads, a generated **tunnel** between the village and the city (`TunnelLayout`: winding three-wide corridor, rest stops, rubble that never closes it, crates, lights, four mob spawners), walking through gates (the user and what they pull), the **storm** (warning with a siren, entry blocked, the location and the tunnel that follows it are deleted and built again with a new layout, everyone inside is gone), staggered timers (6 to 12 hours, an hour apart if the range allows). Commands: `worldbuild [graph] [seed]`, `worldreset <location> [now]`, `worldstatus`, `worldgo <location> <gate>`. Verified with a throwaway engine test: the tunnel can be walked from one gate to the other without digging, a person walks village gate to tunnel to city, the storm deletes them and rebuilds with a new seed.
3. **Laboratory and base** (`f8752f6d40`, next): the tier 3 laboratory is the generated bunker behind an elevator hall with a gate (`BunkerSystem.BuildLaboratory`), with a tunnel to the city; the Military base is a placeholder walled compound of city buildings until the hand-built map file exists (`mapPath`), and a map file can name its own gates. The web is now Village - tunnel - City (- base, - tunnel - Laboratory).
4. **Spawn zone, mobs, loot tiers**: a clearing near the middle of the village with eight spawn points (`SpawnPointHumanError` and `SpawnPointLatejoin`), mob spawners on open ground (8 in the village, 14 in the city, 6 in the base), and loot spawners of the structures take the tier of their location (1, 2, 3).
5. **A round in the world works**: `game.map World` and `errorgate.world.enabled true`. Verified with a throwaway full-round test: the web is built before the players spawn, a connected player spawns in the village next to a spawn point of the zone, and after the round the world is forgotten. The default is off, so the old maps are untouched.
6. **Soak of the whole web** (throwaway test): 6 rounds of building all six locations and wiping the three that reset twice each: about 21,000 to 24,000 entities for the whole web, all entities gone after tearing it down, managed memory up about 3 MB per round (1527 to 1544 MB over six rounds, to watch over a week). A build takes 0.7 to 1.5 s on the main thread, the worst storm 2.2 s: a stall, to be sliced over ticks later.
7. **Invariants of the layout over 300 seeds each** (throwaway probe): no overlaps, nothing on a road or in the sea, fences connect as their rotation says, every front door has a path to a street, every gate and spawn point reachable on foot from the first gate, all roads reachable, shapes within 3 percent of the nominal area.

8. **Builds run over ticks**: the storm no longer stalls the server. The old location is deleted at once, the new one is built in steps under a 6 ms budget per tick (the layout, ground, each structure, batches of about 96 spawned entities) and nobody can enter it before it is finished. Round start, `worldbuild` and the graph build still run in one go. Cancelled with the world when a round ends; a direct build finishes a running one first. Verified with the throwaway engine tests (walk, storm, round, soak). The tunnels, the laboratory and hand-built maps are still built in one step.
9. **Loot caps per location**: the loot manager counted for one map only, so the world (built on an empty map) would have had no loot at all. Every map with a loot manager now keeps its own caps (`LootManagerSystem`), spawners and mob drops draw from their own map's caps, and a wiped location takes its caps with it. A location names its table with `lootTable` (the village, the city, the base and the laboratory use `LootTableGlobal`). Verified with a throwaway engine test: after the build items lie in every location (village 70, city 128, base 48, laboratory 285) and the old loot manager tests still pass.
10. **Roadblocks and a graveyard**: the city has ten roadblocks (two barricades side by side in the middle of the wide avenues; the layout rejects any that would split the roads or sit near a gate, and the probe confirms every gate, road and spawn point stays reachable over 300 seeds), the village has clumps of grave crates. Also a `runWorld` script (`Scripts/bat/runWorld.bat`, `Scripts/sh/runWorld.sh`) that starts the server in the world and the client.
11. **Playtest fixes**: (a) the village air: the ground grid got its own empty atmosphere that filled slowly, so people spawned into a vacuum; outdoor grids now use the map air (full pressure on the first tick, verified: 101 kPa, no damage over 1000 ticks of a real round); gates also refuse while a grid of the location waits for air. (b) the shore follows the shape of the edge. (c) `OreBox` replaces `CrateGenericSteel` in generated maps (bunker, tunnels). (d) roadblocks and city props use N14 metal barricades, sandbags, razorwire and tank traps, no wood. (e) faults on the village and the city. (f) **the laboratory is entered like the bunker**: bunker hatch at the end of the tunnel, key, timed airlock; the key is in the world loot table (1 per location). Verified: no key, the hatch stays shut; with the key the cycle runs and the person arrives in the laboratory; after the laboratory is wiped the new vestibule is joined to the same hatch. (g) **gate plazas**: every outdoor gate stands on a 7 by 5 grid of its own with lamps, hazard marks and barricades; the layout reserves it (300 seeds, all gates and roads still reachable). Cancelling a build (worldclear) deletes its half-built map; the soak test now shows storms cost under 2 ms on the main thread, memory flat, all entities gone after teardown.
12. **Second playtest round**: (a) loot was empty in the world: the location's loot table was set after the loot manager had already started, so it loaded nothing; now set first, every spawner has something to draw (605 in the city, 48 in the laboratory). (b) hatches are named after where they lead (`hatch to snow city`, `bunker hatch to laboratory`), maps and grids have names (`SnowCity map`, `SnowCity ground`, `SnowCity kuz-concrete-L-8`, `SnowCity gate plaza (east)`). (c) tab completion for `worldgen`, `worldclear`, `worldbuild`, `worldreset`, `worldgo`, `structureview`, `structurecheck`. (d) roadblocks close a street completely (road and sidewalk, one or two tiles deep, a mix of the N14 barricades). (e) streets that stop short of each other or meet without a corner are joined (`MendJunctions`). (f) the storm warning: exact time left to everyone in the location, ashfall light, ashfall, heavy, the warning sound is a CVar. **Player-facing text added, to approve:** `world.ftl` (the warning and the countdown; sender MACHINATION OF RUIN; marks at the start, 1:00, 0:30, 10 and 5 seconds; sound `Misc/delta_alt.ogg` with the first and last), the hatch names, the location names `village-city tunnel` and `city-laboratory tunnel`.
13. **Spawn spots near the edge** (like Kuznetsk's seven): the village has seven spots six to sixteen tiles from the edge of the map, each with a round start point and a late join point. The spots keep away from the gates (45 tiles, lowered step by step if the map has no room), from each other (30 tiles, same rule), roads, buildings, the shore and the mobs (20 tiles); the faults already keep 25 tiles from every spawn point. Probe over 300 seeds: seven spots every time, reachable on foot, nothing wrong. The one-ring clearing is gone.
