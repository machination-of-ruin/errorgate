# >>> PRESSURE <<<

## Decision

Pressure, not instructions. Two-key doors, power relays and scarcity need several people. She builds them and logs the result. Betrayal and sharing are both valid. See the premise in [VIBE.md](VIBE.md): MACHINATION OF RUIN never guides, and players keep their free will, which is the error she cannot understand.

## Rules for every mechanism

1. **It never says what to do.** No quest text, marker, hint or objective. The mechanism is physical and players read it themselves.
2. **It needs more than one person, but does not force them to cooperate.** A group can share, split, or let one player take everything and leave the others behind.
3. **Betrayal must be possible.** If a mechanism makes betrayal impossible, it is not pressure, it is a puzzle. Cut it back.
4. **She logs the outcome and does not explain it.** The log line records what happened (who, where, result), never a judgement the players can learn the rules from.
5. **It is not a fair trade.** The reward may be nothing, may be a trap, or may be worth more to one player than to the rest.

## Existing example: the final bunker (EDGE OF ENTROPY)

The final bunker has two separate entrances, each closed by a pair of blast doors. To open them, players follow a chain of control rooms outside the bunker. Any player can open or close those doors, with no ownership. One player can ruin another's plan, or trap others inside a dangerous area completely.

Why it works as pressure:

- It needs several people to move through quickly, and nothing tells them to.
- Betrayal is one switch away and costs nothing, so trust is a real choice.
- Two entrances mean two groups can reach it at once and interfere with each other.
- The control rooms are outside, so the people who open the doors are not the people inside.

Use it as the model for new mechanisms. Reported by the project owner from playtests; verify the layout against the map before building on it.

## Mechanisms (Proposed)

Each is a direction to confirm before building. None of these exist yet.

| Mechanism | What it forces | What betrayal looks like | What she logs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Two-key door | Two switches or keys far apart, both held at the same moment. | The one inside shuts the door on the one holding the switch, or takes what is behind it alone. | Both subjects, door opened or not, who went through. |
| Power relay | A sector stays powered only while someone stands at a relay or keeps it fed. | The relay keeper leaves, or cuts power once the others are inside. | Who held the relay, how long, who was left in the dark. |
| Sealed sector | A door that opens only from outside, or only after a number of subjects are inside. | Locking the others in, or leaving them out. | Entered, sealed, who came out. |
| Shared scarcity | A resource too scarce for the group, such as one medkit, one filter, or the last food in a ruin. | Taking it and leaving. | Who took it and who went without. |
| Weight or count plate | A mechanism held open only by weight or presence. | Stepping off, or removing the weight early. | Who stood, who left, when. |
